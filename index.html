About Quik Delivery LLC

Quik Delivery LLC is an official Amazon Delivery Service Partner (DSP) serving the Napa, CA area. Founded in 2023 by Gurjit Kaur, the company provides last-mile delivery services for Amazon throughout Napa, CA using Amazon-branded vehicles.
